# Haqdaar.ai pitch video

[Haqdaar_Pitch_Video.mp4](Haqdaar_Pitch_Video.mp4): 4 min 05 s, 1920×1080.

1. **The website (0:00–1:48).** A screen recording of the citizen site with the pitch below as
   a voiceover (Sarvam `bulbul:v3`, voice "aditya") and subtitles.
2. **The phone line (1:47–4:05).** A real call to the Haqdaar helpline (080 4729 1722,
   PIN 1234), with the call's own audio and captions of both sides, timed from the API's
   call log. The caller is a 22-year-old student in Punjab looking for help with education.

## What the call shows

PIN 1234 → language menu (English) → "My name is Somraj. My age is 22. I live in city Punjab." →
man → General → help with education → student → ₹20,000 a month → read-back → "You qualify
for 417 schemes, and 22 more may fit" → the two best education schemes: PM Uchchatar Shiksha
Protsahan (central scholarship for college and university students) and the AICTE–Mitacs
Globalink Research Internship.

The scheme counts depend on the corpus at the time of the call (corpus version 3).
